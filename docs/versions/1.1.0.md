# 1.1.0

[All versions](README.md)

A minor version for three things an accessibility audit found against WCAG 2.2 AA. A palette colour used
as text now reads at 4.5:1 on the page and a surface in every variant, where MudBlazor's fell as
low as 1.17:1; dark mode's secondary text reaches 4.5:1 on a surface; and a field's resting
underline or outline reaches the 3:1 non-text contrast it needs to be found.

## What changed

### Text

- **A colour as text reads at 4.5:1.** A link, a text or outlined button, a text or outlined
  chip, a toggle item, a selected tab, list row, tree row or nav link, a focused or invalid field's
  label, a field's error message, a text or outlined FAB or avatar, and MudText's colour classes
  painted the palette colour itself, which on the page or a surface, or on the colour's own 6%
  tint under a text button or a selected row, fell short of 4.5:1 for 12 of 16 colours: dark
  primary links were 2.67:1, light success 2.13:1, dark `dark` 1.17:1. They now paint a new
  `Fili{Colour}TextColor` (and its brush): the colour itself where it already reads (primary and
  dark in light, tertiary and warning in dark, every high-contrast colour), and otherwise the
  alert text 1.0.1 introduced, the same hue darkened in light and lightened in dark to 4.5:1. The
  colour stays the fill and the line, so a filled button, its border and an outlined button's
  border are unchanged. A deliberate departure from MudBlazor, named beside each value in
  `Palette.axaml`.

- **Dark secondary text reads at 4.5:1.** `FiliTextSecondaryColor` in dark was MudBlazor's white
  at 50%, 4.26:1 on the dark surface; it is white at 53% (`#87FFFFFF`), 4.57:1 on the surface and
  4.75:1 on the page. Placeholders, helper text, field labels at rest and every other
  text-secondary in dark are a little lighter.
- **A text or outlined snackbar's text is the alert text.** It was still MudBlazor's darken shade,
  as an alert's text was before 1.0.1 (light warning 2.87:1 on the surface); it is now
  `Fili{Colour}AlertTextColor`, as a text or outlined alert's is.

### Field edges

- **A field's resting edge reaches 3:1.** `FiliLinesInputsColor`, the underline of a standard or
  filled field and select and the outline of an outlined one, was MudBlazor's `#BDBDBD` in light,
  1.88:1 on white, and white at 30% in dark, 2.50:1 on the surface; WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of what
  shows where a control is. It is `#848484` in light (3.74:1 on white, 3.05:1 on a filled field's
  fill) and white at 43%, `#6DFFFFFF`, in dark (3.52:1 on the surface, 3.06:1 on the fill). An
  outlined default chip's border and the forked base's mid border, which are lines-inputs too,
  darken with it. High contrast was already `#E8E8EE` and is unchanged.
- **A default avatar keeps its ground.** It painted lines-inputs, as MudBlazor's does, and
  text-primary on the darker grey would fall to 2.7:1, so it paints a new
  `FiliAvatarBackgroundColor`, MudBlazor's lines-inputs value in each variant.

### Menus

- **The first menu an app opens from the keyboard tints its row.** Since 1.0.1 a menu tints its
  focused row only when a key opened it, which the window learns from its key and pointer
  handlers; they were registered as the first menu's theme was built, after the key that opened
  it, so that menu showed no row. They are now registered as the theme loads.
  `MenuHighlightTests` passed only when another menu test had run first.

New keys, all published and frozen in the baseline: `FiliPrimaryTextColor`,
`FiliSecondaryTextColor`, `FiliTertiaryTextColor`, `FiliInfoTextColor`, `FiliSuccessTextColor`,
`FiliWarningTextColor`, `FiliErrorTextColor`, `FiliDarkTextColor` and
`FiliAvatarBackgroundColor`, each with its `*Brush`. `TextContrastTests` and
`InputLineContrastTests` hold every value, and the real controls, to 4.5:1 and 3:1 in all three
variants.

## Upgrading from 1.0.1 to 1.1.0

No markup changes. Text in a colour looks different where the table above names it, dark
secondary text is a little lighter, and a field's resting underline or outline is darker in
light and dark.

An app that overrides a palette colour sets its new text key too, in each variant, or that
colour's text keeps the shipped value: override `FiliPrimaryColor` alone and its links and text
buttons stay the theme's purple. Pick a text value that reads at 4.5:1 on your page and surface;
the theme's own are the colour where it does and that hue darkened or lightened until it does.
An app that set `FiliLinesInputsColor` or `FiliTextSecondaryColor` keeps its values, though its
default avatars no longer follow lines-inputs: it sets `FiliAvatarBackgroundColor` for them. One
that has to keep MudBlazor's values sets the keys back to them in
its application's `Resources`, per variant, knowing they fall short of AA.
