# 0.18.0

[All versions](README.md)

A nav label in a `DockPanel` or `Grid` written straight into a row's `Header` wraps, as 0.17.0 said
it would; it ran on at one line, cut off at the drawer's edge. Only string headers and templated
ones wrapped. The date picker's toolbar fits its 100px again: 0.17.0 gave its 34px date a button's
1.75 line, 72px, where `.mud-button-date` is on a 1.17 line, 52px, and the year and date overflowed
the toolbar. A command bar button with its label on the right is a button's 36.5px, where it stayed
29. And the toggle icon buttons carry no label line box, which DrawerPage's pane toggles, wearing
them without the `icon` class, had kept. A `ToggleButton`, MudToggleItem, is a MudButton's 36.5px
too, where it was 32. The docs now say that overriding a colour means its hover, darken, lighten and
contrast-text tokens as well. See [Upgrading from 0.17.0 to 0.18.0](#upgrading-from-0170-to-0180).

## Upgrading from 0.17.0 to 0.18.0

- **A `ToggleButton` is 36.5px** (30.75 small, 42.25 large), where it was 32: MudToggleItem is a
  MudButton, on its 1.75 line. A toggle group sized round the old height grows by 4 or 5px.
- **A command bar button with its label on the right is 36.5px**, where it was 29.
- **A nav label in a `DockPanel` or `Grid` written straight into the `Header` wraps**, on 28px
  lines, as 0.17.0 said it would. A menu that relied on it being cut off now has taller rows.
- **Overriding a colour is five tokens.** If your app sets `Fili{Color}Color` alone, its hover,
  press and filled-hover states are still MudBlazor's: add `…HoverColor`, `…DarkenColor`,
  `…LightenColor` and `…ContrastTextColor` (see [Theming](https://filiarrochada.github.io/Fili.Theme.MudAvalonia/#theming) on the docs site).
