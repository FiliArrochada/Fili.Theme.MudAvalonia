# 0.25.0

[All versions](README.md)

Five of MudBlazor's options that apps had been building by hand, every docs page checked against
MudBlazor 9.11, and a theme that costs less to apply. Nothing changes without a new class, save the
looks transcribed back to MudBlazor's; the published class list grows to 183
([Upgrading](#upgrading-from-0240-to-0250)).

## What changed

### New options

- **`clearable`** on a text field, an autocomplete or a number field is MudInput's `Clearable`: a
  small clear button while the field holds text that is not blank and is neither disabled nor
  read-only, before an end adornment and out of the Tab order. On a date picker it clears the date
  at once, as MudPicker's Clear does, where the picker would read the emptied text only on losing
  focus.
- **`loading`** on a data grid is MudDataGrid's `Loading` and `LoadingProgressColor`: an
  indeterminate 4px bar in its own row under the header, info, or in the grid's colour class.
- **`subheader`** on a `TextBlock` is MudListSubheader: action-default, medium 14px text, 8px above
  and 20px below, with `inset` and `no-gutters` for Inset and Gutters="false".
- **`check-mark`** is MudChipSet's and MudToggleGroup's `CheckMark`: on a selectable chip, MudBlazor's
  check while it is selected, in place of its start icon; on a toggle item, before its content.
- **An `AutoCompleteBox` has MudAutocomplete's drop-down arrow**: ArrowDropDown in an edge-end icon
  button, ArrowDropUp while open, right against the text as `.mud-select-input` has it, and clicking
  it opens and closes the list. `MinimumPrefixLength` is 0, MudAutocomplete's `MinCharacters`, so
  an empty field lists everything.

### Fixes

- **The date picker's button and the autocomplete's arrow are raw to automation**, the picker's own
  peer expanding.
- **A checkbox in a `DataGrid` column header** is the table's compact one, as one in a cell is:
  `.mud-table-cell .mud-checkbox` covers header cells too.
- **An `overlay` whose class is removed** gives its content its own alignment back, where it stayed
  centred and kept a stale entry for each time the class came and went.
- **A striped progress bar's bands stand still with `FiliReducedMotion`**, the indeterminate bar
  still moving.
- **A menu longer than the screen** - a context menu, a flyout's or a submenu - scrolls in its
  sheet, as `.mud-popover` does, where it was measured in full and ran off the edge.

### Looks transcribed back to MudBlazor 9.11

Every docs page is checked against MudBlazor 9.11: a parity row for every parameter that changes a
component's look or behaviour, Missing with a reason where nothing here does it, and the component
index's overall parity to match. These looks had drifted:

- scroll bars as MudThemeProvider's 8px grey rail (new `FiliScrollbarThumb*` tokens);
- nav links 14px on a 24.5px line, with an ArrowDropDown group arrow;
- small icon buttons, a data grid's sort button among them, 26px round a 20px icon;
- the alert's 24px icon;
- the switch thumb at elevation 1;
- a filled snackbar's message at weight 400;
- tooltips centred on a 1.4em line;
- a dense numeric field's 20px spin column;
- the grey halo on an `inherit` checkbox and radio;
- a selected chip's ripple;
- `TextBlock.button`'s 24.5px line;
- tabs always uppercase.

### Performance

- **On a typical screen the theme attaches 45% fewer styles**, allocates a fifth less and handles
  hover in half the time. A colour class now sets its colour once for the template to read, instead
  of a style per colour, part and state, and no style is matched against every control.
- **The theme loads a quarter faster**: the styles that switch a button, chip, field, badge or nav
  menu to its variant's control theme no longer build that theme up front.
- **Ripples:** a control builds its ripple's canvas on its first press rather than when it is
  shown, a typical screen building 5% fewer visuals.
- **List and tree rows** are built without the multi-selection checkbox unless the list has
  `multi-selection`: 200 list rows allocate a quarter less and build a fifth faster.
- **A list's colour class** sets its selected row's colour on the list for its rows to inherit,
  where three styles per colour reached every row: a list row carries a quarter fewer style entries
  and allocates 8% less.
- **A text field builds its helper line** only when it has helper text, an error or `counter`, and
  its clear button only with `clearable`: 200 fields build a third faster in 40% less memory, and a
  typical screen 14% faster. A select builds its helper line the same way.
- **Only a `dense` data grid's cells** look for the grid's last column.
- **The docs say** that publishing with ReadyToRun cuts the theme's first load to a third.

### Docs site

- **A Custom components page:** MudAlert's default icon per severity, MudAvatarGroup's Max and its
  "+n" avatar, closable chips, MudDynamicTabs and MudDataGrid's SelectColumn, built in the app as
  worked examples with their C#.
- **The Control states page is gone**, every sample on it now on its component's page, with new
  examples for Color.Inherit buttons, a binding's validation error and toggles outside a group.
- **The Avatar page's group example is MudAvatarGroup's:** a -12px overlap, 44px and 60px ringed
  avatars, the first on top. Its styles, like the Max example's, keep a small, rounded or square
  avatar's size and shape, and the Max slider reaches 0, as MudBlazor's does.

## Upgrading from 0.24.0 to 0.25.0

No class or key is renamed or removed. `clearable`, `loading`, `subheader` and `check-mark` are
new words in the published class list: grep your app for them before upgrading, as an app's own
style of that name now meets the theme's. `FiliScrollbarThumbColor`, `FiliScrollbarThumbHoverColor`
and their brushes are new keys. These look or behave differently, as MudBlazor's do:

- **Every `AutoCompleteBox` shows a drop-down arrow** at the end of its field, a 48px icon button
  that opens and closes the list, and the text runs 8px closer to any end adornment. A search box
  that should not read as a drop-down sets `InnerRightContent="{x:Null}"`, or every field at once
  with `<Style Selector="AutoCompleteBox"><Setter Property="InnerRightContent" Value="{x:Null}" /></Style>`.
  Content of the app's own in `InnerRightContent` replaces the arrow as before; to bring the
  arrow back after replacing it, clear the value rather than set null. A theme `BasedOn` the
  autocomplete's inherits the arrow.
- **An empty `AutoCompleteBox` lists everything** when opened, by the arrow, Down or F4:
  `MinimumPrefixLength` is now 0, where Avalonia's default is 1. Set it back to 1 on a field to
  list nothing until something is typed; the arrow then does nothing over an empty field.
- **A `CheckBox` in a `DataGrid` column header is the compact table checkbox**: the 32px circle
  (28 small, 44 large) with -4px margins, as one in a cell has been, where it was the 48px one.
- **Scroll bars are 8px**, MudThemeProvider's injected rail, where they were 12: content that
  scrolls gains 4px of width. The thumb is a fixed grey, `FiliScrollbarThumb*`, darker on hover
  and while dragged, in every variant but high contrast.
- **A nav menu row is 40.5px**: its link is the default 14px type on a 24.5px line, where it was
  body1 on 28 (44px). A group's arrow is ArrowDropDown, MudNavGroup's default; an app that
  swapped it through `FiliExpandMoreGeometry` in a group's resources sets
  `FiliArrowDropDownGeometry` instead.
- **Tab labels are always uppercase**, as `.mud-tab` is: `FiliButtonTextTransform` no longer
  reaches them.
- **Small controls grow by 2px**: a small icon button is 26px round a 20px icon (the clear button,
  a small toggle icon button and the snackbar's close button with it), and a tooltip's line is
  1.4em, centred, so its chip is 25px. An alert's icon is 24px, its height unchanged. A data grid's
  sort button is the same small icon button: its arrow grows to 20px and the button shrinks to 26px.
- **Card parts and `material-elevation-overlay` name the controls they go on.** A card part
  (`card-header`, `card-header-avatar`, `card-header-content`, `card-header-actions`,
  `card-content`, `card-actions`) takes a `StackPanel`, `DockPanel`, `Grid`, `WrapPanel`,
  `Border`, `TextBlock`, `Button` or `Image`, and `card-actions` centres its items on a panel;
  `material-elevation-overlay` takes a window, a `UserControl`, one of those panels, a `Border`, a
  `ScrollViewer` or a `ContentControl`. On any control they were styles matched against every
  control in the window, 40% of a screen's style instances with the avatar's and the toolbar's
  child styles. A part on another control loses its margin and docking: wrap it in a `Border`, or
  put the class on the panel round it.
- **A text field's helper line and clear button are no longer parts of its template.** An app's
  style that reached into them with `/template/` - `TextBox /template/ Grid#PART_HelperLine`,
  `/template/ TextBlock#PART_HelperText`, `/template/ Button#PART_ClearButton` - no longer
  matches. `PART_HelperLine` is now a `ContentPresenter`, which `/template/` still reaches for
  the line's margin and colour; the helper text, counter, error and clear button are built inside
  it, or inside `PART_ClearHost`, on a field that shows them: reach them with a descendant
  selector, `TextBox TextBlock#PART_HelperText`. A select's helper line has changed the same way:
  `ComboBox TextBlock#PART_HelperText`.
- **Quieter details**: the switch thumb's shadow is elevation 1, a filled snackbar's message is
  Regular weight, a dense numeric field's spin column is 20px, an `inherit` checkbox or radio
  keeps the grey hover halo, and `TextBlock.button` is a 24.5px line.
