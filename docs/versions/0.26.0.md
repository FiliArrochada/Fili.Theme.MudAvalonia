# 0.26.0

[All versions](README.md)

Lighter templates: fewer visuals in list rows, checkboxes, radios and switches, and an
indeterminate progress bar that no longer re-lays out the window. Nothing looks different; some
template parts are renamed ([Upgrading](#upgrading-from-0250-to-0260)).

## What changed

- **Lighter list rows, checkboxes and radios.** A list row draws its padding on its content
  presenter rather than a panel round it, and a checkbox or radio scales its glyph for `small` and
  `large` with a render transform rather than a `LayoutTransformControl`. Building 200 takes about
  15% less time for list rows and 9% less for checkboxes and radios.
- **One glyph Path per checkbox.** It swaps its glyph and colour with the state, instead of three
  Paths with two hidden. Building 200 takes about a quarter less time and memory. A radio keeps its
  two glyphs.
- **A switch's thumb icon has no Viewbox.** The icon's Path is scaled to the thumb less 2px a side,
  per size, as before. Building 200 switches takes about a tenth less time and memory.
- **An indeterminate progress bar measures only itself.** Its two bars grow and shrink as
  MudBlazor's do, and a bar longer than the rail made the rail,
  the progress bar and every control round it measure again on each frame. 60 bars in a desktop
  window take about a quarter less CPU, and look and move exactly as before.
- **Docs site: a performance toggle** in the app bar draws Avalonia's FPS, layout and render graphs
  over the site, with the page's visual count and build time. A hint for spotting a page that works
  on every frame, not a benchmark.
- **Docs site: the examples show again.** The trimmed browser build had dropped the compiled XAML
  each example is loaded from, so every one failed with "No precompiled XAML found".

## Upgrading from 0.25.0 to 0.26.0

No class or key is renamed or removed, and nothing looks different. Some template parts have
changed, which matters only to an app's own style that reaches into a template with `/template/`:

- **A checkbox draws its glyph with one `Path#PART_Glyph`.** `PART_Unchecked`, `PART_Checked`,
  `PART_Indeterminate` and `PART_Scale` are gone from a `CheckBox`'s template: a selector such as
  `CheckBox /template/ Path#PART_Checked` names `Path#PART_Glyph` instead, with `:checked` or
  `:indeterminate` on the checkbox for one state's glyph.
- **A radio's `PART_Scale` is a `Panel`** scaled by a render transform, where it was a
  `LayoutTransformControl`: a selector naming `LayoutTransformControl#PART_Scale` names
  `Panel#PART_Scale`.
- **A list row has no `DockPanel`** unless its list has `multi-selection`: its padding is on its
  content presenter.
- **A switch's thumb icon has no `Viewbox`**: `PART_ThumbIcon` is the thumb's child, scaled by
  its `RenderTransform`, which the size classes set; a style that sets the icon's
  `RenderTransform` replaces that scale.
