# 1.0.1

[All versions](README.md)

A patch for two things accessibility reviews found. A text or outlined alert's text now reaches
WCAG AA's 4.5:1 on its own ground in light and dark, where MudBlazor's darken shade fell as low
as 1.4:1; and a menu opened with the pointer no longer highlights its first row.

## What changed

- **Alert text reaches 4.5:1 in every variant.** `Fili{Colour}AlertTextColor` (and its brush)
  was MudBlazor's darken shade in light and dark, which on the alert's 6% tint, over the page or a
  surface, fell short of 4.5:1 for 13 of the 16 colours: light warning was 2.87:1, light info
  3.82:1, dark primary 1.87:1, dark `dark` 1.41:1. Each that fell short keeps its hue and is
  darkened in light, lightened in dark, to the first value at 4.5:1 with a level to spare for how
  Skia rounds the tint; primary and dark in light, and every high-contrast value, already passed
  and are unchanged. A deliberate departure from
  MudBlazor, named beside each value in `Palette.axaml`. No key is added, renamed or removed.

  | Light | secondary | `#FF1F69` | `#E1004A` | 3.46 to 4.55 |

- **A menu opened with the pointer highlights no row.** A menu flyout or context menu focuses and
  selects its first row when it opens, and the theme tinted a selected row, so a click showed the
  first row highlighted, beside the item that was actually ticked. A row is now tinted on hover,
  and where the keyboard is: in a menu opened from the keyboard, or once an arrow key is pressed
  in it, as MudBlazor's are.

## Upgrading from 1.0.0 to 1.0.1

Nothing to do. An app that set its own `Fili{Colour}AlertTextColor` or `…AlertTextBrush` keeps
its values. An app relying on the old look sees darker alert text in light and lighter in dark,
for the colours above; one that has to keep MudBlazor's darken shade sets the key back to it in
its application's `Resources`, per variant, knowing it falls short of AA.
