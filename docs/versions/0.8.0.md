# 0.8.0

[All versions](README.md)

The tabs' sliding indicator and MudDataGrid's nested groups. No new classes; three things look
different ([Upgrading](#upgrading-from-07-to-08)).

## What changed

- **The tab indicator slides**, as MudTabs' does, over from the old tab to the new one, on a
  `TabControl` or a `TabStrip`, on any side.
- **Nested `DataGrid` groups:** a nested group's header steps in 48px per level and its rows stay
  put.
- **A `dense` grid's last visible column** has 16px on its right, as MudDataGrid's does.
- **Fix:** a group row's 1px stray edge is gone.

## Upgrading from 0.7 to 0.8

Nothing needs to change in markup; three things look different.

- **The tab indicator slides** between tabs instead of fading, over 0.3s. There is no class to
  turn it off.
- **A `dense` `DataGrid` gives its last column 8px less** on the right, so an auto-sized last
  column is 8px narrower and a star-sized column takes the difference.
- **Grouped `DataGrid`s:** with more than one group level, the nested group headers move in 48px
  per level. Every group row's title moves 1px left, where a stray row-header edge had pushed it.
