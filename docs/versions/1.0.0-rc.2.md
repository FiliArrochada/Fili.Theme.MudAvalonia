# 1.0.0-rc.2

[All versions](README.md)

The second release candidate for 1.0, on Avalonia 12.1.5 and MudBlazor 9.12: the forked templates
are Avalonia 12.1.5's, and the package needs it; the look follows MudBlazor 9.12, which adds a
divider with content and styles the text avatar. A command bar's overflow now scrolls, and a date
picker opened with the pointer no longer draws the keyboard ring.

## What changed

### Avalonia 12.1.5

- **Avalonia 12.1.5 is the minimum.** The forked templates are Avalonia Simple's at 12.1.5, and
  the package depends on `Avalonia` 12.1.5 or later, below 13. Of the fork, only CommandBar
  changed. `Avalonia.Controls.DataGrid` is still themed from 12.1.0.
- **A command bar's overflow scrolls.** A long overflow menu scrolls up and down, as a menu does,
  rather than running off the screen: Simple's does from 12.1.5, and the theme's own did not.
- **A date picker opened with the pointer shows no keyboard ring.** From 12.1.5 a picker's
  popover takes the focus when it opens, so its arrow keys work; the ring round the day they are
  on is drawn once a key is pressed there or in the picker that opened it, not on a click, which
  had drawn it over today's ring.

### MudBlazor 9.12

- **A divider with content: `divider` on a `ContentControl`.** MudDivider's new ChildContent, the
  content between two lines in the divider colour, 12px either side of it; `vertical` puts the
  lines above and below, and `light`, `inset` and `middle` work as on a `Separator`. A new class,
  so an app that already puts `divider` on a `ContentControl` of its own picks up its look.
- **A `text` avatar has no ground.** 9.12 gives `Variant.Text` a style of its own: the initials in
  text-primary, or in the colour a colour class names, where 9.11's unstyled avatar was white on
  gray-light. `FiliAvatarBareTextColor` and its brush, the white it used, stay published and
  resolve as before, but the theme no longer paints with them.
- **`MudText.Truncate` is `TextTrimming="CharacterEllipsis"`**, an Avalonia property, so nothing
  is added; its parity row says so.

## Upgrading from 1.0.0-rc.1 to 1.0.0-rc.2

Raise every `Avalonia*` package to 12.1.5 or later, together: an app pinned to an older 12.1
patch fails restore with NU1605, a package downgrade.

A `text` avatar changes look: from white on gray-light to its text on no ground. One that has to
keep the old look takes the background and foreground itself:
`Background="{DynamicResource FiliGrayLightBrush}"` and
`TextElement.Foreground="{DynamicResource FiliAvatarBareTextBrush}"`.
