# 0.20.0

[All versions](README.md)

Six gaps the apps found moving onto 0.19.0. A `PathIcon` draws its glyph as MudIcon does, in a 24px
viewBox scaled to its size, where Avalonia's stretched the glyph's bounds to the box: icons are
smaller and sit where Material put them, and an app no longer pads its glyphs. An alert holds
`.mud-alert-message`'s 9px, so a one-line alert is MudAlert's 50px, and its icon is 22px, 12px
before the message, on the first line. The newest snackbar is at the bottom of the stack wherever it
is anchored, as MudBlazor's, where a bottom stack put it at the top; `newest-on-top` is
`NewestOnTop`. A field with no `PlaceholderText` takes no room for a label: a standard field is
MudInput's 32px. `list-item` on a Button is MudListItem with `OnClick`, a row that acts. And
`caption` and `overline` have no colour of their own, as in MudBlazor: they were grey, which also
turned a caption link grey. The published class list grows to 171. See [Upgrading from 0.19.0 to
0.20.0](#upgrading-from-0190-to-0200).

## Upgrading from 0.19.0 to 0.20.0

- **Icons are smaller.** A `PathIcon` keeps a Material glyph's padding, as MudIcon does. Padding
  you added to make a glyph fill 24×24 can go: the glyph draws in a true 24px box now. A curved
  glyph you padded with move commands comes out a little smaller than in 0.19.0, because a
  curve's control points widened its bounds and 0.19.0 drew it about 10% too large. A glyph drawn
  in another box needs scaling into 24.
- **Captions and overlines are the colour round them**, not grey. Where you want grey supporting
  text, set `Foreground="{DynamicResource FiliTextSecondaryBrush}"`.
- **An alert is taller**: 50px for one line, where it was 32.
- **A text field with no `PlaceholderText` is shorter**: a standard one is 32px, where it was 48.
  Give it a label, or a `MinHeight`, to keep the old height.
- **Snackbars at the bottom stack the other way**: the newest at the bottom. `newest-on-top` on
  the manager puts it back on top.
- **Two new class names**, `list-item` and `newest-on-top`: grep your app for them.
