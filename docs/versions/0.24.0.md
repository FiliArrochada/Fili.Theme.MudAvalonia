# 0.24.0

[All versions](README.md)

MudPopover's options on flyouts and context menus, and MudCarousel's arrow and bullet positions.
Nothing changes without a class, save a carousel's bullets. The published class list grows to 179
([Upgrading](#upgrading-from-0230-to-0240)).

## What changed

- **Flyouts, menu flyouts and context menus** take MudPopover's `Elevation`, `Square`, `DropShadow`
  and `Paper`: `elevation0` to `elevation25`, `square`, `flat` and the new `no-paper`, through
  `FlyoutPresenterClasses` on a flyout and on the `ContextMenu` itself.
- **`material-elevation-overlay`** follows the popover's level, and leaves a `flat` or `no-paper`
  sheet alone.
- **Carousel arrows**, MudCarousel's `ArrowsPosition`: `arrows-top` and `arrows-bottom` move them
  from the middle.
- **Carousel bullets**, MudCarousel's `BulletsPosition`: `bullets-top` and `bullets-center` move
  them from the bottom. They sit in a
  48px band, as MudCarousel's bullet buttons do, so they are on the arrows' line when both share an
  edge, and a carousel's bullets move 12px up from the bottom.

## Upgrading from 0.23.0 to 0.24.0

No class or key is renamed or removed. `no-paper`, `arrows-top`, `arrows-bottom`, `bullets-top`
and `bullets-center` are new words in the published class list, and `elevation0` to
`elevation25`, `square` and `flat` now reach a flyout's presenter and a context menu: grep your
app for them before upgrading, as an app's own style of that name now meets the theme's. One
thing looks different, as MudBlazor's does:

- **A carousel's bullets** sit 12px higher, centred in a 48px band as MudCarousel's bullet
  buttons are, so they share the arrows' line when both are on the same edge.
