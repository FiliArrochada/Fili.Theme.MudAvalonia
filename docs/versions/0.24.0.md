# 0.24.0

[All versions](README.md)

MudPopover's `Elevation`, `Square`, `DropShadow` and `Paper` on a flyout, a menu flyout and a
context menu, which were missing: `elevation0` to `elevation25`, `square`, `flat` and the new
`no-paper`, through `FlyoutPresenterClasses` on a flyout and on the `ContextMenu` itself.
`material-elevation-overlay` follows the level, and leaves a `flat` or `no-paper` sheet alone.
Nothing changes without a class. And MudCarousel's `ArrowsPosition` and `BulletsPosition`:
`arrows-top` and `arrows-bottom` move the arrows from the middle, `bullets-top` and `bullets-center`
the bullets from the bottom. The bullets sit in a 48px band, as MudCarousel's bullet buttons do, so
they are on the arrows' line when both share an edge, and a carousel's bullets move 12px up from the
bottom. The published class list grows to 179. See [Upgrading from 0.23.0 to
0.24.0](#upgrading-from-0230-to-0240).

## Upgrading from 0.23.0 to 0.24.0

No class or key is renamed or removed. `no-paper`, `arrows-top`, `arrows-bottom`, `bullets-top`
and `bullets-center` are new words in the published class list, and `elevation0` to
`elevation25`, `square` and `flat` now reach a flyout's presenter and a context menu: grep your
app for them before upgrading, as an app's own style of that name now meets the theme's. One
thing looks different, as MudBlazor's does:

- **A carousel's bullets** sit 12px higher, centred in a 48px band as MudCarousel's bullet
  buttons are, so they share the arrows' line when both are on the same edge.
