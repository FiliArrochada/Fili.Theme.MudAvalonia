# 1.2.0

[All versions](README.md)

A minor version for the rest of the WCAG 2.2 AA audit: what a palette colour paints as a fill or
as a line. Text on a filled colour now reads at 4.5:1 in every variant, where MudBlazor's white
fell to 1.93:1; a checked box, a switch, a slider, a focused underline and every other line or
glyph in a colour reaches the 3:1 non-text contrast it needs; and a colour's text keeps 4.5:1
under the pointer too.

## What changed

### Fills

- **Text on a bright fill is black.** A filled button, chip, FAB, toggle item, alert, snackbar,
  badge, avatar, tooltip, tab bar or app bar in secondary, tertiary, info, success, warning or
  error painted MudBlazor's white text, as low as 1.93:1 on dark warning and 2.13:1 on tertiary.
  Their `Fili{Colour}ContrastTextColor` is black at 87% (`#DE000000`) in light and dark, Material's
  text on a light fill, which reads at 4.60:1 or more on the fill and on its darken shade under the
  pointer. Primary and dark keep white. The fills themselves are MudBlazor's.

  | Variant | Colour | White | Now |

  | Light | tertiary | 2.13 | 6.29 |
  | Light | info | 3.12 | 4.70 |
  | Light | success | 2.24 | 5.69 |
  | Light | warning | 2.16 | 6.20 |
  | Light | error | 3.68 | 4.60 |
  | Dark | secondary | 3.33 | 5.19 |
  | Dark | tertiary | 2.13 | 6.29 |
  | Dark | info | 2.94 | 5.27 |
  | Dark | success | 2.51 | 5.28 |
  | Dark | warning | 1.93 | 6.82 |
  | Dark | error | 3.40 | 4.89 |

- **Dark primary is a shade darker.** `FiliPrimaryColor` in dark was MudBlazor's `#776BE7`, whose
  white text is 4.17:1, and black on it would fall to 3.29:1 on its darken shade under the pointer.
  It is `#6F62E6`, the same hue darkened to the first value where white reaches 4.5:1 with a level
  to spare: 4.60:1, 6.58:1 on its darken shade. Its darken, lighten and hover shades follow it by
  MudBlazor's rule (`#5141E1`, `#8E83EC`, `#0F6F62E6`), and so does the forked base's accent.
- **A coloured tab bar's hovered tab darkens.** The active tab of a tab bar in a colour took the
  colour's lighten shade under the pointer, as MudBlazor's does, and white text on primary's
  lighten is 4.17:1. It takes the darken shade, as a filled button's hover does, so its contrast
  text reads at 4.5:1 in every colour.

### Lines and glyphs

- **A colour as a line or glyph reaches 3:1.** A checked checkbox or radio, an on switch's thumb,
  a slider's thumb and track, a progress bar, a focused or invalid field's underline or outline,
  a tab's indicator, an active nav link's line and icon, an open nav group's arrow, a select's
  coloured arrow, an alert's icon, a coloured `PathIcon`, a multi-selection list's checkboxes, a
  data grid's loading bar, invalid cell and drop indicator, and a text field's caret painted the
  colour itself: a light tertiary checkbox was 1.74:1 on the page and dark primary 2.14:1 on a
  filled field, short of WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1. They paint a new `Fili{Colour}NonTextColor` (and its
  brush): the colour itself where it reaches 3:1 on the page, a surface, a filled field's fill and
  the colour's hover tint over either (primary, error and dark in light; tertiary, info, success
  and warning in dark; every high-contrast colour), and otherwise the same hue darkened in light
  and lightened in dark to 3:1. The colour stays the fill, and the border of an outlined control,
  which its text identifies. A slider's value label stays a chip of the colour with its contrast
  text.

  | Variant | Colour | MudBlazor | Now | Worst ratio |

- **A toggle item with no colour class has primary text.** Its theme painted `FiliPrimaryColor` as
  its text; it paints `FiliPrimaryTextColor`, as a `primary` one does.

### Text under the pointer

- **A colour's text reads at 4.5:1 on a hovered chip or nav link.** A text chip and a selected
  outlined chip lay the colour at 12% under their text on hover, and a selected nav link deepens
  its tint by another 6.4%; text chosen on the resting 6% tint fell to 4.2:1 there. Every
  `Fili{Colour}TextColor` that fell short is a step further from the ground, so it reads at 4.5:1
  on those tints too. They are no longer equal to the alert text, which stays on its 6% tint.

  coloured text falls below 4.5:1 there (2.9:1 in dark); that needs a different hover look, not a
  token, and is left for a later version.

New keys, all published and frozen in the baseline: `FiliPrimaryNonTextColor`,
`FiliSecondaryNonTextColor`, `FiliTertiaryNonTextColor`, `FiliInfoNonTextColor`,
`FiliSuccessNonTextColor`, `FiliWarningNonTextColor`, `FiliErrorNonTextColor` and
`FiliDarkNonTextColor`, each with its `*Brush`. `FillContrastTests` and `NonTextContrastTests` hold
every value, and the real controls, to 4.5:1 and 3:1 in all three variants, and `TextContrastTests`
now reads hovered chips.

## Upgrading from 1.1.0 to 1.2.0

No markup changes. Filled controls in secondary, tertiary, info, success, warning and error have
black text, dark primary is a shade darker everywhere it fills, and checkboxes, radios, switches,
sliders, progress bars, focused underlines, tab indicators and coloured icons are darker in light
and lighter in dark where the table above names them.

An app that overrides a palette colour sets its new non-text key too, in each variant, or that
colour's lines and glyphs keep the shipped value: override `FiliPrimaryColor` alone and its checked
boxes and focused underlines stay the theme's purple. Pick a value that shows at 3:1 on your page,
surface and filled fields; the theme's own are the colour where it does and that hue darkened or
lightened until it does. Check its `ContrastTextColor` reads at 4.5:1 on the colour and its darken
shade, and its `TextColor` on the colour at 12%. One that has to keep MudBlazor's values sets the
keys back to them in its application's `Resources`, per variant, knowing they fall short of AA.
