# 0.19.0

[All versions](README.md)

A nav label with a type class (`caption`, `body2`...) keeps its class's line height, as
`.mud-typography-*` overrides the line it would inherit from `.mud-nav-link`; the nav's 28px line
was forced on it, so a caption under a title grew the row. Labels still wrap. The docs add
`FiliActionDefaultHoverColor` to what overriding `FiliActionDefaultColor` needs, and warn that
`FiliButtonTextTransform` set on a button reaches its flyout: a single label keeps its case as a
run. A wrapped nav group keeps its arrow on the first line, as `.mud-nav-link`'s `align-items:
flex-start` does. DrawerPage's pane toggles are MudIconButton's 48px circle, where the fork's bar
squeezed them to a 48x38 pill. And the spinner `DatePicker`'s highlight follows `FiliPrimaryColor`,
where it stayed MudBlazor's purple. `Color.Inherit` reaches three more controls: `inherit` on a
`TextBlock` is the surrounding colour over a type class's own, and on a checkbox or radio the
glyph's, with `unchecked-inherit` for `UncheckedColor`. MudSelect's `AdornmentColor` is
`adornment-{colour}` and `adornment-inherit` on a `ComboBox`. A chip with nothing to click, MudChip
with neither `OnClick` nor `Href`, is `chip` on a `ContentControl`: no hover, ripple or focus. The
docs show how to change one control's hover and ripple through tokens in its `Resources`, rather
than by reaching into its template. The published class list grows to 169. See [Upgrading from
0.18.0 to 0.19.0](#upgrading-from-0180-to-0190).

## Upgrading from 0.18.0 to 0.19.0

- **Ten new class names**: `unchecked-inherit`, and `adornment-primary` … `adornment-dark` with
  `adornment-inherit`. Grep your app for them before upgrading, as for any published class.
- **A `ContentControl` with the class `chip` is now a chip.** If your app styles its own
  `ContentControl.chip`, the theme's chip look now applies to every property your style does not set.
- **A nav label with a type class keeps that class's line height**, so a `caption` under a title
  in a nav row is shorter than in 0.18.0.
- **`DrawerPage`'s pane toggle is a 48px circle**, and its top and bottom bars lose their 5px
  above and below.
- **If a style of yours reaches a template part** to change a control's hover or ripple, it can
  now be tokens in that control's `Resources` instead (see [Theming](https://filiarrochada.github.io/Fili.Theme.MudAvalonia/#theming) on the docs site).
