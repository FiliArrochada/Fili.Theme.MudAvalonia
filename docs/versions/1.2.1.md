# 1.2.1

[All versions](README.md)

A patch that closes the one gap 1.2.0 left in its text contrast. A selected list row or select
option under the pointer turned grey and its coloured text fell to 2.9:1 in dark; it now keeps
its colour, a little deeper, and its text reads at 4.5:1 in every variant.

## What changed

### Text under the pointer

- **A selected list row or select option keeps its colour under the pointer.** It swapped its
  tint for the pressed overlay, and its coloured text fell below 4.5:1 there (2.9:1 in dark).
  Its tint now deepens with the colour at 6.4%, as a selected nav
  link's does, on which every colour's text token already reads at 4.5:1. A row of a
  `multi-selection` list, which marks no row selected, is unchanged.

## Upgrading from 1.2.0 to 1.2.1

Nothing to do: no markup changes and no new keys. A selected list row or select option under the
pointer shows its colour slightly deeper instead of grey. A list whose theme is an app's own, not
based on the theme's, sets `Accent.RowFill` beside `RowColour` and `RowHover`, or its selected
row shows only its tint under the pointer.
